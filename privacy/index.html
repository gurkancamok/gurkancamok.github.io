This portfolio

This portfolio uses static pages, local assets and small scripts for mobile navigation and the awards gallery. It does not contain a contact form, advertising scripts or analytics added by the portfolio developer.

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and server logs under its own policies. Professional enquiries can be made through the linked professional profiles, subject to those services’ own policies.

External applications and links

Project applications and professional profiles are separate destinations with their own behaviour and policies. External links open in a new tab.

The public advisor-matching demonstration uses browser-local storage and fictional advisor data. Do not enter confidential information into public demonstrations.

Clinical and research scope

Case studies describe software development and its evidence. They do not provide patient-specific medical advice. Clinical decisions remain subject to professional judgement, institutional governance and the relevant source protocol.

Research prototypes are identified as such. The website does not claim regulatory clearance, NHS deployment or established patient-outcome improvements for them.
