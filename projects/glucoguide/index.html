Case study / medication education

GlucoGuide Clinical

A browser-based oral antidiabetic medication resource designed for clear, accessible and structured patient education.

Stage: Public education applicationMy role: Designer and developerUpdated October 2026

The education need

People receiving diabetes medication need accessible explanations of use, common side effects and when to seek professional advice. A browser resource can be opened from a shared link or QR code without requiring a separate app.

My contribution

I structured the medication information, organised the educational content, designed the interface and implemented the web application.

  • Medication selection through a simple browser interface.
  • Consistent presentation of medication-specific educational information.
  • Use-related guidance, side effects and advice to seek professional review.
  • Mobile-compatible access and QR-link distribution.

Technology: HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages.

Current evidence scope

The public application demonstrates implementation and access to educational content. Improved adherence, reduced medication errors or reduced clinical workload have not been established by the evidence presented here.

Future evaluation can examine comprehension, accessibility, content review, usage and patient-reported usefulness.

Clinical content boundaries

GlucoGuide provides general education. Patient-specific treatment decisions remain with the treating healthcare professional. Medication content requires periodic review against authoritative prescribing and product information.

A browser-local interaction count, if displayed, is not an institutional usage statistic or a measure of patient benefit.
