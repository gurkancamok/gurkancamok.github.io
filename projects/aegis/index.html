AEGIS Clinical Risk Intelligence Engine

A multi-domain clinical risk prototype exploring score normalisation, service-context weighting and nonlinear risk amplification.

The computational concept

AEGIS brings NEWS2, Glasgow Coma Scale, Morse Fall Scale, Braden Scale, RASS and glycaemic risk inputs into a shared computational interface.

It explores service-specific weighting and amplification of co-occurring risk signals, rather than displaying each score in isolation.

My contribution

I implemented the application structure, input organisation, score normalisation, service-context logic, composite risk presentation and report-generation workflow.

  • Multi-domain score normalisation.
  • Context-dependent weighting by clinical service.
  • Explicit risk-amplification logic.
  • Risk profile display and domain-level visualisation.
  • PDF report generation in the demonstration.

What is and is not validated

The established source scales and the AEGIS composite are different constructs. Combining established scales does not validate the resulting global risk index, thresholds or recommendations.

The composite requires independent development and validation work before clinical interpretation or deployment. Use the public prototype only to inspect the software concept with synthetic inputs.

Next evaluation priorities

Define the intended use and reference outcomes; examine weighting and threshold choices; test calibration, discrimination and error propagation; and evaluate performance across services and patient groups.

No demonstrated reduction in adverse events, diagnostic accuracy, regulatory clearance or clinical deployment is claimed.
