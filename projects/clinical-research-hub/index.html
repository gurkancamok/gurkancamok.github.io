Case study / research workflow software

Clinical Research Hub

A shared web workflow for research-topic creation, discovery, selection and availability across clinical teams.

Stage: Institutionally deployed — in hospital useMy role: Software developerUpdated October 2026

On this page

The coordination problem

Research and quality-improvement ideas need a shared place where teams can discover available topics, see their clinical relevance and record ownership. A shared workflow makes this information easier to keep consistent than separate messages and lists.

My implementation

I implemented the browser interface and Firebase-backed shared data workflow for topic creation, text search, clinical-specialty and project-type filters, named selection and availability counters.

  • Create and organise research and quality-improvement topics.
  • Filter by specialty and project type.
  • Find topics through text search.
  • Record topic selection and show remaining availability.

The advisor-allocation system is a separate product. Its matching logic and operational figures are described in its own case study.

Reported institutional use

The Clinical Research Hub is deployed and in use at the hospital. Project records prepared in October 2026 describe approximately 50 topics, 30 selections and access for around 400 staff. These are reported operational figures, not an independently audited activity dashboard.

The evidence demonstrates software applied to a shared organisational workflow. Access does not establish active usage by every eligible staff member.

Scope and further development

The workflow supports coordination, topic visibility and selection. It does not by itself establish improved research quality, publication output or patient outcomes.

Further evaluation can examine time to selection, duplicate-topic avoidance, user adoption, project completion and the quality of the shared research pipeline.

Supporting records

Institutional screenshots and authorised operational confirmation can be provided for appropriate professional review. Internal user identities and research records are not published on this portfolio.
