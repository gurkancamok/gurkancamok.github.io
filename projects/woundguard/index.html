Case study / AI-supported research prototype

WoundGuard

A pressure-injury image-classification and treatment-guidance prototype, with separate functions for image assessment and clinician-reviewable guidance.

Stage: Prototype; external clinical performance not establishedMy role: Technical co-developer and product leadUpdated October 2026

On this page

The product conceptMy technical contributionExternal project recognitionEvaluation still requiredSupporting project records

The product concept

WoundGuard explores image-assisted pressure-injury assessment alongside an explicit guidance workflow. Classification and treatment guidance are separate functions so that each can be evaluated and the consequences of an incorrect classification can be examined.

My technical contribution

As technical co-developer and product lead, I designed and developed the technical integration bringing the image-analysis component and clinician-reviewable guidance into a clinical decision-support workflow.

The supporting TÜSEB Technology Transfer Office submission identifies me among the developers. This is attribution evidence; it is not represented as a granted patent or clinical certification.

External project recognition

In 2025, an independent jury ranked the project third in a competition for innovative digital products in health transformation, held within the international health sciences congress in Istanbul.

This recognises the project in a competition setting. It does not establish clinical accuracy or effectiveness.

Evaluation still required

  • Representative datasets and a clearly defined reference standard.
  • Independent external clinical validation across institutions.
  • Assessment across different skin tones and image-acquisition conditions.
  • Separate evaluation of classification and downstream guidance.
  • Clinician usability, oversight and risk evaluation before clinical deployment.

The portfolio does not claim proven clinical effectiveness, regulatory clearance or routine institutional deployment for this prototype.

Supporting project records

Developer-attribution and competition records can be provided for appropriate professional review. There is no public clinical-use application linked from this portfolio.
