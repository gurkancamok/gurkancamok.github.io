Evidence & implementation

Make the logic visible.
Make the evidence inspectable.

Evaluation is part of product development. Each claim should have a defined method, context and limit.

  1. 01

    Clinical rules translated into code

    Source-protocol ownership is distinguished from my responsibility for software implementation, testing and evaluation.

  2. 02

    A reference standard calculated independently

    Two blinded endocrinologists established the insulin study’s reference rates. Digital outputs matched in all 120 evaluated cases.

  3. 03

    A defined institutional deployment

    The insulin assistant was approved for routine institutional use and made available across intensive care, emergency and inpatient units.

  4. 04

    Transparent boundaries

    Protocol concordance, task timing, user adoption and patient outcomes are separate questions. Prototypes are labelled by their actual stage.

Featured evaluation

120 paired cases.
A clearly defined result.

The insulin evaluation used recorded clinical inputs in a prospective shadow design. Manual calculations informed treatment; digital calculations were performed the following day and did not influence care.

The study supports concordance with the protocol in the evaluated sample. Independent-user usability, external validation and clinical outcomes require further evaluation.

About

Product development.
Technical ownership.

I design and build digital health products, taking ideas from problem definition to working software and real-world evaluation.

I am a hands-on software developer with a clinical background. Since 2023, I have developed a portfolio spanning clinical decision support, AI-supported wound-assessment research, medication education and research workflow systems. My clinical experience helps me identify problems and understand users; my contribution is the design and development of the products that address them.

I take technical ownership across product definition, requirements analysis, application architecture, interface design, coding, testing and evaluation. Depending on the project, I work as sole developer, creator and developer, or technical co-developer and product lead. My work includes decision algorithms, explainable matching logic, shared data workflows and usable web applications.

The portfolio includes systems deployed in hospital practice, publicly accessible applications and clearly identified research prototypes. I also contribute to the wider digital health conversation through published articles, invited speaking and research. My next development focus is collaboration with clinical teams and technology partners to independently evaluate these products and extend their use in new settings.

Development approach

From a clinical problem
to an evaluated product.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the decision.

Identify the user, source rules, information gaps and workflow constraints before defining the product.

02 / IMPLEMENT

Build for review.

Translate requirements into visible logic, clear inputs, useful outputs and explicit exception paths.

03 / EVALUATE

Measure in context.

Define the reference, test conditions, users and deployment boundaries. Keep observed results separate from intended benefits.
