Insulin study: interpretation boundaries

The developer entered recorded inputs into the application the next day. Digital outputs did not influence treatment. Independent clinical reference calculations were produced by two endocrinologists.

The developer also conducted the study, which is a relevant methodological limitation. The evaluation demonstrates protocol-reference concordance within its sample; it does not establish general clinical superiority.

The manual/digital timing comparison used different workflow conditions. Independent-user usability, bedside workload and patient outcomes require separate evaluation.

Supporting professional-review records

Study materials, institutional approval and adoption records, developer-attribution documentation and competition records can be supplied where appropriate for professional review. Internal records are not published wholesale on this website.

The final independent technical review is pending. Its agreed scope concerns implementation, rule execution, safety controls, validation methods and personal contribution. Technical originality and wider implementation potential were outside the agreed review scope.

The insulin manuscript was submitted for journal review. Neither submission nor a draft review is represented as a completed peer-reviewed publication or final endorsement.

Engineering transparency

The public repositories expose implementation and explanatory documentation. Technical review should distinguish the currently published build, the study build and the institutional production version.

Version-specific specifications, boundary cases and regression evidence are priorities for ongoing development. This portfolio’s website checks concern navigation, responsive layout, page structure and assets; they are not clinical algorithm validation.

Wider deployment and future work

External pilots, new partnerships, multicentre studies and UK development are future plans unless a completed activity is explicitly documented. No NHS partnership or deployment is claimed.

Future evaluation should define intended users, outcomes, source protocols, reference standards, revision history and oversight before broader clinical use.
