Professional profile

I am a digital health software developer with a clinical background, designing and building products across clinical decision support, AI-supported wound-assessment research, medication education and research workflows.

Since 2023, I have taken projects through product definition, requirements analysis, application architecture, interface design, coding, testing and evaluation. My roles range from sole developer and product creator to technical co-developer and product lead. The portfolio includes hospital-deployed systems, public applications and research prototypes.

My clinical practice, diabetes case management and evidence-based research leadership at Anadolu Medical Center in Türkiye, an institution affiliated with Johns Hopkins Medicine, provide practical insight into users, workflows and evaluation needs.
